How to Choose the Perfect Formal Shoes

Formal shoes can make or break your professional look. From Oxford to Derby, Monk Strap to Loafer — understanding the difference helps you dress sharper at every occasion...

Formal shoes can make or break your professional look. Oxford shoes are the most formal — closed lacing, sleek toe, perfect for black-tie events. Derby shoes have open lacing and are slightly more casual, great for office wear. Monk Strap shoes add a stylish buckle detail, ideal for business-casual settings. Loafers are slip-on and versatile — from smart-casual to semi-formal. Always choose genuine leather for durability. Match shoe color to your belt. Black shoes go with navy, grey, and black suits. Brown shoes pair beautifully with tan, beige, and brown trousers.

Running Shoes Guide: Find Your Perfect Fit

Choosing the wrong running shoes can lead to injuries. Pronation type, arch support, cushioning level — we explain everything you need to know before buying your next pair...

Choosing the wrong running shoes can lead to injuries. First, understand your pronation: neutral, overpronation, or supination. Neutral runners can wear most shoes. Overpronators need stability shoes with medial post support. Supinators need extra cushioning. Check your arch type — flat, normal, or high. Flat feet need motion control shoes. High arches need maximum cushioning. Replace running shoes every 500-700 km. Top picks: ASICS Gel-Nimbus for neutral runners, Brooks Adrenaline GTS for overpronators, Nike React Infinity for injury prevention. Always try shoes in the evening when feet are slightly swollen for the best fit.

How to Care for Your Shoes & Make Them Last

A good pair of shoes is an investment. With proper care, your favourite pair can last years. We share expert tips on cleaning, storing, and maintaining all types of footwear...

A good pair of shoes is an investment. For leather shoes: wipe with a damp cloth, apply leather conditioner monthly, use shoe trees to maintain shape. For sneakers: remove laces and insoles before washing, use mild soap and a soft brush, air dry — never machine dry. For suede: use a suede brush to remove dirt, apply suede protector spray. Store shoes in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Rotate your shoes — wearing the same pair daily breaks them down faster. Use cedar shoe trees to absorb moisture and odour. Polish leather shoes regularly to maintain shine and prevent cracking.
